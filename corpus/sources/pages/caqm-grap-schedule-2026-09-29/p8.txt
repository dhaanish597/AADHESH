 
 CITIZEN CHARTER 
• Check AQI from SAMEER App and stage of GRAP in force from CAQM website. 
• Keep engines of your vehicles properly tuned. 
• Maintain proper tyre pressure in vehicles.  
• Keep PUC certificates of your vehicles up to date.   
• Do not idle your vehicle, also turn off the engine at red lights.  
• Do not ply end-of-life / visibly polluting diesel or petrol vehicles. 
• Prefer public transport or pooling of vehicles for commuting. 
• Prefer EVs to control vehicular pollution. Ensure segregation and proper disposal of 
municipal solid waste. 
• Do not litter / dispose waste, garbage in open spaces. 
• Ensure disposal of waste only in designated collection points provided by municipal 
authorities especially in markets and public places. 
• Ensure immediate removal of C&D waste and compliances  of dust mitigation 
measures. 
• Plant more trees and cover open spaces with grass and shrubs. 
• Avoid using DG sets except for essential activities. 
• Celebrate festivals in an eco-friendly manner – avoid firecrackers. 
• Report air polluting activities through 311 App, Green Delhi App, SAMEER App etc. 
• Participate in Information Education and Communication (IEC) activities and spread 
awareness about air pollution mitigation measures among fellow citizens through 
various means including social media channels.
